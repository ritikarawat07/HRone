## 2. Examined and Found Correct

- **Employee Creation `_id` Stripping:** The starter stripped `_id` via `doc.pop("_id", None)` prior to returning. This pattern was preserved and fortified with explicit response model projections.
- **IST Timezone Constant:** The starter defined an IST timezone offset of `UTC+05:30`. The constant was retained and consistently applied across all instant-to-date conversions and overnight calculations.
- **Modular Route Structure:** The starter's route layout and FastAPI application instantiation were preserved and cleanly extended within [`app/main.py`](file:///c:/Users/ASUS/OneDrive/Documents/HRone/app/main.py) without breaking backward compatibility.
